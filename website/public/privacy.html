JSTorrent Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

This privacy policy applies to JSTorrent across all platforms: the Chrome extension, desktop app (Windows, Mac, Linux), and Android app.

Data Collection

JSTorrent does not collect any personally identifiable information. The application does not have access to your browsing activity.

Analytics

JSTorrent does not transmit analytics or usage data to any external service.

Network Activity

JSTorrent connects to BitTorrent trackers and peers as part of normal torrent downloading and seeding operations. The extension communicates with the desktop companion app or Android companion app on your local device. No data is sent to JSTorrent's servers.

Stored Data

Torrent metadata and settings are stored locally on your device. No data is synced to the cloud unless you explicitly configure cloud storage as a download location.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the JSTorrent GitHub page.
